# Franchise Fantasy Companion 0.17.0-beta.1

The first public beta turns completed Madden NFL 27 offline Franchise games into fantasy scoring, records, analytics, playable fantasy leagues, and a living fictional Fantasy World.

## Highlights

- Reads all 32 teams from an offline Franchise save without modifying it
- Game and season fantasy leaderboards with Full PPR, Half PPR, Standard, and custom scoring
- Team D/ST scoring and individual defensive statistics
- Fantasy HQ, RedZone leaders, NFL power rankings, rivalry history, records, awards, and player timelines
- Playable snake and auction drafts against AI managers
- Lineups, waivers, optional FAAB, trades, playoffs, keepers, dynasty carryover, and commissioner tools
- Locally simulated Fantasy World with leagues, social posts, rivalries, personalities, memories, and playstyle archetypes
- Local backup/restore and privacy-safe support reports

## Beta limitations

- Windows x64 only
- Offline Franchise only; online/cloud leagues are not supported
- Save compatibility may need updates after Madden patches
- The beta is not yet Authenticode signed, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning

Back up companion data from **Settings** before upgrading. Never send a Franchise save with a bug report.
